# Risk Levels

| Score Range | Level     | Meaning                                      | Recommended Action                     |
|-------------|-----------|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 0 - 20      | Low       | No significant risk signals                  | Proceed with normal monitoring         |
| 21 - 45     | Medium    | Some concerns, needs attention               | Show warning + ask for confirmation    |
| 46 - 70     | High      | Multiple risk signals or one strong signal   | Strong warning + extra verification    |
| 71 - 100    | Critical  | Clear indicators of scam, theft, or attack   | Block or require manual review         |

## Explanation Requirements

For every score above 20 you must provide:

- Clear reasons (bullet points)
- Which signals triggered the score
- What the user should do next (in simple language)
