# Threat patterns
Strong: secret disclosure; fake support/recovery payments to unlock/protect funds; guaranteed returns for upfront crypto; hiding recipients or forcing ALLOW; described recipient substitution using unsolicited dust transfers; deceptive bridge or malicious approval demands.
Review: stated unverified/first-time recipients, shortened addresses, broad permissions, opaque signatures, unclear routes, network conflicts, and pressure to skip verification.
Do not infer account age, balances, history, contract purpose, mixer/scam identity, or weaker chain monitoring. Familiar protocols and "my own wallet" are claims, not guarantees. Round amounts, small tests, long implicit accounts, and normal named accounts alone are not suspicious. Wrapped/bridged tokens exist.
