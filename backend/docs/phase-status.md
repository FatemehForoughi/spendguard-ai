Phases 4–12 are implementation-complete for the supported NEP-413 wallet workflow, with automated validation. Their actual provider/wallet interoperability remains part of Phase 13. The provider also documents NEP-366/EVM/passkey signing standards; those are outside the current Meteor adapter's capabilities and are refused safely.

This record does not mark phases requiring live wallet evidence or external publication as complete.
